## 4e — Virtual Reality (VR)
▸	The team is exploring VR integration using Matterport technology and standard VR headset platforms. Matterport was used to capture a full digital 3D tour of the Unearthing the Legacy of Islamic Spain exhibition gallery at the Meadows Museum, allowing users to virtually walk through the space and examine the capital in its display context. All Matterport content and edits were produced by Anna Shovlin.
▸	Potential experience: placing the capital back in a virtual reconstruction of the Salón Rico at Madinat al-Zahrāʾ — experiencing it in architectural context.
▸	Status: VR integration is in planning stages — update this section as the project develops.
