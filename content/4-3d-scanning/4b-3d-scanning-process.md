## 4b — The 3D Scanning Process
▸	Technology: EinScan HX2 hybrid structured-light / laser scanner — produces high-resolution mesh models.
▸	Process: The capital was scanned at the Meadows Museum during the Unearthing the Legacy of Islamic Spain exhibition. The scanning team included JT Ringer (DIG Lab Manager, SMU), Anna Shovlin (MA student, UT Dallas), and Nida Jaffer (MA student, UT Dallas). Multiple scan passes from different angles were aligned and merged to capture the deeply undercut surfaces. A complementary Matterport scan of the gallery space was also captured by Anna Shovlin to provide spatial and exhibitionary context.
▸	Output: Full OBJ mesh model + texture maps. Team is working to produce an optimized smaller OBJ for web/VR deployment.
▸	Challenges: The deeply undercut acanthus leaves and the capital's multi-directional surfaces required careful repositioning to capture occluded areas.
