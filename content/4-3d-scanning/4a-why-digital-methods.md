## 4a — Why Digital Methods?
▸	Traditional photography cannot capture the full three-dimensionality of a carved capital — a 3D model allows 360° examination of carving depth, undercutting, and surface texture.
▸	The digital scan creates a permanent, non-contact record — a resource for conservation, future scholars, and institutions without physical access to the object.
▸	Foundation for a larger database of dispersed Madinat al-Zahrāʾ capitals worldwide (see Section 5).
