## 2b — Madinat al-Zahrāʾ - The Brilliant City
▸	Founded 936 CE on the slopes of the Sierra Morena above Córdoba — three terraces of palaces, mosques, gardens.
▸	Name meaning: 'most radiant city' or 'shining city' — architectural manifesto of caliphal legitimacy.
▸	Key spaces: the Salón Rico (Rich Hall / Throne Hall of ʿAbd al-Raḥmān III), administrative quarters, mosque, gardens.
▸	Destruction: the fitna of 1009–1010 — civil war, sacking, and the city's disappearance under farmland for ~1,000 years.
▸	Today: UNESCO World Heritage Site; active archaeological excavation; on-site museum.
▸	Key source: Vallejo Triano, A. (2015), 'Madinat al-Zahrāʾ: Historical Reality and Present-Day Heritage.'
