## Purpose
This section provides the historical and art-historical framework for readers encountering al-Andalus and Madinat al-Zahrāʾ for the first time, while still offering depth for specialists through citations and footnotes. It situates the capital within Umayyad caliphal ambition, architectural rhetoric, and the material culture of medieval Iberia.
