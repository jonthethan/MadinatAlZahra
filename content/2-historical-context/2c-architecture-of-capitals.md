## 2c — The Architecture of Capitals - Rhetoric in Stone
▸	The Corinthian capital as a Mediterranean form reactivated and transformed by Umayyad craftsmen.
▸	Caliphal capitals: dense acanthus foliage, corner volutes, interlaced vegetal and geometric motifs — distinct from Roman precedents.
▸	The reuse of Roman spolia: thousands of antique columns imported from Ifriqiya (Tunisia/Algeria) and Tripolitania (Libya) — hybrid materiality.
▸	The inscription as architectural element: Kufic blessings on the abacus — invocations of divine authority woven into structure.
▸	Scholarly debate: Cressier, P. (2010) on the role of the capital in caliphal architectural discourse; Cressier, P. (1995) on the Rich Hall capitals.s
