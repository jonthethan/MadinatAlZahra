## 3c — Roman Spolia and the Question of Recarving
▸	One research question: Was this capital originally a Roman Corinthian capital that was recarved by Umayyad craftsmen, or is it a wholly new creation?
▸	Evidence for recarving: proportional analysis; structural anomalies visible in 3D scan; comparison with known reused Roman capitals at Madinat al-Zahrāʾ.
▸	Significance: The reuse of Roman spolia was a deliberate caliphal strategy — appropriating the visual authority of Rome while asserting Islamic sovereignty. See Calvo Capilla (2014), 'The Reuse of Classical Antiquity in the Palace of Madīnat al-Zahrāʾ.'
