## 3b — The Kufic Inscription (feature content)
This is a major scholarly contribution of the project. Prof. Ali Asgar Alibhai (UT Dallas) has produced a complete reading and translation of the inscription. Present this as an annotated, sequential walkthrough of all nine inscription segments.
▸	Complete translation: 'In the name of God the Merciful. Blessed is He who, if he willed, would make for you God benevolence. Blessings from God. Complete Happiness.' (Alibhai, 2024)
▸	Transliteration: Bism Allāh al-Raḥīm, Tabāraka alladhi ʼin shāʼa jaʻala laka khayr Allāh. Baraka min Allāh. Ghibṭa Kāmila
▸	Note that some letters are no longer legible due to damage — Alibhai's report specifies which segments are partially reconstructed.
▸	Contextualize the blessing phrases: ghibṭa kāmila ('complete happiness') is a well-attested ornamental phrase appearing on Andalusi ivories, metalwork, and carved ivories such as pyxides.
