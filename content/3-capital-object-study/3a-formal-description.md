## 3a — Formal Description
▸	Describe the capital's physical form: deeply carved acanthus leaves on the drum, four corner volutes, interlaced vegetal and geometric motifs, string of gem-shaped motifs along the upper drum.
▸	Describe the abacus: Kufic inscriptions on all four faces — angular Arabic script carrying Quranic blessing phrases.
▸	Material: white marble — likely local Spanish quarry, though the column shaft it once stood on may have been imported spolia.
▸	Condition notes: some damage to inscription legibility (noted in Prof. Alibhai's translation report).
